# Glossary

**The five factors**: moral influence, weather, terrain, command and doctrine; the fixed checklist for comparing both sides before committing to anything ([Chapter 1](chapters/01-laying-plans.md)).

**Moral influence**: genuine support for an effort from the people carrying it out, distinct from mere compliance ([Chapter 1](chapters/01-laying-plans.md)).

**Doctrine**: the organisation, roles and supply behind an effort; whether the structure holds up, not just whether the plan sounds right ([Chapter 1](chapters/01-laying-plans.md)).

**Stratagem**: winning by disrupting an opponent's plan or support before any direct, costly confrontation becomes necessary ([Chapter 3](chapters/03-attack-by-stratagem.md)).
