# Human Review Checklist

Run through this before relying on the generated files.

- [ ] The index stays short (roughly 100 lines or under) and points to the glossary and techniques document rather than repeating them

- [ ] For a policy or training course, any contradiction between sections was flagged, not silently resolved by picking one side
- [ ] You have the legal right to use this source this way, and the output stays private, not published or redistributed
- [ ] At least one section file has been skimmed against the original before relying on it for something that matters
