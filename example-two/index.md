# Larchmont Group Remote Work Policy: Index

**Source:** Internal HR policy, two sections.

**What it covers:** Office attendance requirements, and a later update to those requirements for specific roles.

## Sections

1. **Attendance (Jan 2024):** a flat three-day-per-week office minimum for all staff, exceptions need director sign-off.
2. **Remote Work Update (Jun 2025):** confirmed remote-eligible staff may work fully remote instead.

## Contradiction Flagged

Sections 1 and 2 genuinely conflict on the surface, three days minimum versus fully remote. This is not an unresolved conflict: Section 2 is later and more specific, and explicitly states it supersedes Section 1 for eligible roles only. For staff not confirmed as remote-eligible, Section 1's three-day minimum still applies unchanged.

**Working rule:** check remote-eligible status first. If confirmed eligible, Section 2 governs. If not, Section 1 governs. Do not apply Section 2 as a blanket replacement for Section 1, it explicitly is not one.

## Glossary

**Remote-eligible:** a role status confirmed by a manager, not a default; this is the fact that decides which section actually applies to a given member of staff.
