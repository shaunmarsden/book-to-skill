# Techniques and Patterns

Every named method across the three structured chapters, with its source.

| Technique | What It Is | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The five-factor comparison | Compare both sides honestly against moral influence, weather, terrain, command and doctrine before committing to anything | [Chapter 1](chapters/01-laying-plans.md) |
| Cost-of-delay check | Weigh the compounding cost of a long effort against the cost of acting decisively now, since delay is expensive on its own | [Chapter 2](chapters/02-waging-war.md) |
| The ranked-options approach | Before any direct confrontation, check whether the opponent's plan, then their alliances, can be disrupted first; treat a direct confrontation as the fallback, not the goal | [Chapter 3](chapters/03-attack-by-stratagem.md) |
| Mutual-understanding check | Before acting, check how well you understand your own side against how well you understand the other side; that comparison predicts the outcome better than confidence does | [Chapter 3](chapters/03-attack-by-stratagem.md) |
