# Source Material: Larchmont Group Remote Work Policy

An entirely fictional internal HR policy, two sections that genuinely conflict on the surface.

## Section 1: Attendance (issued January 2024)

All staff are required to work from the office a minimum of three days per week. Exceptions require director-level sign-off.

## Section 2: Remote Work Update (issued June 2025)

Following a company-wide review, staff in roles confirmed as remote-eligible by their manager may work fully remote. This supersedes the minimum office attendance requirement in Section 1 for eligible roles only. Staff not in a confirmed remote-eligible role remain subject to the three-day minimum in Section 1.
